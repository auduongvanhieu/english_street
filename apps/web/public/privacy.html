English Street — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

English Street is a language-learning game. This page explains what the web game and the mobile app collect and why.

What we store

  • Account data: a guest id generated on your device, or your email and a hashed password if you register. Your display name is visible to other players.
  • Learning data: conversations with the AI shopkeepers, corrections, vocabulary notebook, quest results, level test answers and progress (XP, coins, streak). These make the game work and let you review your progress.
  • Chat: text you send to other players is relayed live and is not stored, except the last lines attached to a report you or another player submits.
  • Voice: speech recognition runs on your device or in your browser; only the recognised text is sent to our server. Voice chat between players is peer-to-peer (WebRTC) and never recorded.
  • Usage analytics: anonymous events such as page loads, frame rate, quest starts and settings changes, to improve the game.
  • Push notifications (mobile app): a device token so we can send reminders. You can disable notifications in your system settings.

Who processes it

Conversations with shopkeepers are generated by Anthropic's Claude API; the text of your messages in those conversations is sent to Anthropic to produce the reply and is not used to train models. Everything else stays on our servers in Vietnam.

Children

The game is intended for learners aged 13 and over. Players can report or mute others at any time.

Your choices

You can play as a guest without an email. To delete your account and data, write to hello@coffeesos.online.
